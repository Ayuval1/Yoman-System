- [stated] asked to remember as a guiding rule: study no more than one hour straight, then a 10-minute screen-free break (sport/movement, reading a book, a snack, sitting in the sun), then another hour, and so on
- [stated] The 10-minute break is a minimum (taken from a book he read as what works best), not a fixed length; breaks are usually a bit longer, and a break that includes his physiotherapy routine runs 30–45 minutes
- [stated] Deep study for six and a half hours straight is not possible; two sessions with a break between them works, and the break can be his strength/mobility routine
- [stated] At most about three hours a day of focused work, with study and homework counted together
- [stated] A normal break (without physio) is usually 10–15 minutes; he tries to stay off the phone and not scroll Instagram during it
