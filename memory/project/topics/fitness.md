- [stated] Active runner participating in a triathlon fitness group called 4fitness
- [stated] Does physiotherapy exercises at home on his own
- [stated] Runs four times a week: two 4fitness sessions plus two on his own
- [stated] 4fitness on Monday and Wednesday from 18:00, strength training straight after until 20:00; leaves home at 17:30 by bike
- [stated] 4fitness usually ends between 19:45 and 20:00; he sometimes stays up to 15 minutes after to sit with friends before riding home
- [stated] There is no independent run before a 4fitness session
- [stated] Own run Tuesday afternoon, roughly 18:30–19:30
- [stated] Own run Friday morning: up at 04:30, runs from home at 05:30 (warm-up to a friend's house by 05:45, then ~1h15 — about 1.5h total, lately continuous), then sea and a café; no podcasts on this run
- [stated] Tuesday run is from home, no travel; listens to podcasts/courses on the bike ride to and from 4fitness and on the Tuesday run, often switching between several at once
- [stated] Iron rule in his words: "ריצות לא מוותרים בחיים וגם אימונים - ספורט מעל הכל" — holds even in a week with three exams
- [stated] Dislikes a day without training; a day with training beats an otherwise identical day without it
- [stated] No recovery cost after a long run or hard session — the next morning feels fine
- [stated] After an evening session he can't study or do homework needing focus; only light logistics or computer work
- [stated] Wants about 30 minutes of strengthening, stretching, foam roller and mobility scheduled at times he specifies — this is the same thing as his home physio exercises; not on Monday, Wednesday or Friday
- [stated] Physio slots: Sunday 17:00–17:30, Tuesday 19:30–20:00 (right after the run), Thursday 20:30–21:00
- [stated] Saturday is a full rest day for the body (no training, no physio), at least for the current training period; studying is still fine on Saturday
- [stated] Tuesday 18:00–18:30 is spent getting ready for the run
- [stated] Monday and Wednesday: everything else stops at 17:00; 17:00–17:30 is spent getting ready before leaving for 4fitness at 17:30
- [stated] Records runs and workouts with a Garmin watch and a phone app
- [stated] Uses TrainingPeaks; his coach's planned workouts there also show up in his Garmin Connect calendar
- [stated] The coach who plans his workouts in TrainingPeaks is the same 4fitness coach; the weekly 4fitness schedule arrives on WhatsApp Saturday evening, and the coach enters the week's workouts (reaching Garmin) only on Monday afternoon
